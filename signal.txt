Category: CREDIT
Headline: Global borrowing costs surge to pre-crisis highs
What happened:
Global borrowing costs climbed to fresh highs as bond yields surged, driven by higher oil prices, persistent inflation and AI-related investment shifts, raising financing costs for governments and corporates worldwide.
Why it matters:
Higher global borrowing costs tighten financial conditions, compress equity multiples, raise refinancing burdens and increase sovereign fiscal deficits, forcing central banks to reconcile inflation control with growth risks.
Second-order effects:
- Corporate profit margins pressured via higher interest expense
- Emerging-market outflows heighten sovereign funding stress
- Equity valuation multiples likely to compress further
Markets to watch:
US 10-year yield, UK 10-year gilt, ICE BofA US High Yield Spread, Brent oil
END STORY

Category: MACRO
Headline: BoE and FCA outline vision for tokenisation in wholesale markets
What happened:
Bank of England and the FCA published a shared vision to accelerate tokenisation of UK wholesale markets, promoting digital issuance, settlement pilots and regulatory frameworks to modernise post-trade infrastructure.
Why it matters:
Tokenisation could shorten settlement cycles, lower issuance costs, expand investor access and reshape liquidity provision, altering demand for custody, trading platforms and post-trade service providers.
Second-order effects:
- Growth opportunity for digital custody and infrastructure providers
- Pressure on legacy clearinghouses and CSDs
- Regulatory arbitrage risk across jurisdictions increases
Markets to watch:
UK 10-year gilt, LSEG shares, Coinbase (COIN), GBP/USD
END STORY

Category: FX
Headline: Dollar slips as markets trim Fed rate-hike expectations
What happened:
The US dollar eased against the euro as markets reduced odds of further Fed rate hikes, reacting to softer data and shifting inflation and growth expectations.
Why it matters:
A weaker dollar alters capital flows, eases dollar funding strains, lifts commodity prices and boosts emerging-market currencies and equities sensitive to USD movements, and corporate FX exposures.
Second-order effects:
- Commodity exporters gain pricing power
- EM local-currency bond inflows increase
- Hedging costs for multinationals may decline
Markets to watch:
EUR/USD, DXY index, US 2-year yield, EMBI Global
END STORY

Category: MACRO
Headline: UK job vacancies hit five-year low as small firms halt hiring
What happened:
UK job vacancies fell to a five-year low as smaller firms scaled back recruitment, signaling softer labour demand amid higher borrowing costs and uncertain business outlook.
Why it matters:
Cooling vacancies suggest easing wage growth and services inflation, reducing pressure on the Bank of England to hike rates and influencing consumer spending and housing demand.
Second-order effects:
- May slow wage-driven consumption growth
- Supports lower short-term gilt yields
- Eases urgency for further BoE tightening
Markets to watch:
UK CPI services, UK unemployment rate, UK 2-year gilt, GBP/USD
END STORY

Headline: Revolut targets Australia's cut‑throat mortgage market expansion
What happened:
Revolut announced plans to expand into Australia’s mortgage market, leveraging digital distribution and competitive pricing to capture retail mortgage share from incumbent banks and scale quickly.
Why it matters:
Entry intensifies competition in mortgages, pressuring margins for Australian lenders, influencing mortgage pricing, securitisation volumes and deposit strategies and could accelerate fintech-led disruption in household lending.
Second-order effects:
- Spurs rate cuts from incumbent lenders
- Boosts mortgage-backed securities issuance
- Draws tighter APRA regulatory focus
Markets to watch:
Commonwealth Bank (CBA.AX), Westpac (WBC.AX), Australia 10-year yield, AUD/USD
